# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people
- Welcome diverse viewpoints and backgrounds

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Doxxing or publishing private information
- Sustained disruptive or bad-faith behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject content and contributions that violate this policy.
